Monolith
   Range (      ): 3 → 5


We felt that games were ending way to quickly, and so are toning down gold and gem
income per camp. The listed numbers are so low as we plan to alter the value of camp
groups through the use of Gem and Gold clusters as we can add clusters to increase the
groups gem or gold value without increasing clear di"culty by much.


We've noticed some camps getting cheesed by Stellar, Prism, Ryth and other champions
that have long ranged damage and have thus decided to increase the range of some
camps to nerf this form of farming.



Golem (new)
   Health (     ): 5000

   Strength (     ): 120

   Range (      ): 4

   Gold (     ): 160

   Gems (      ): 36

   Respawn (       ): 80 rounds


Monolith has been very di"cult to fight and often too hard to work towards on some maps,
so we are adding Golem as a bridge between the regular camps and Monolith.




Kill reward
   Gold (     ): 50 → 100


We want to incentivise more aggressive strategies such as invades and contesting camps,
and to do this we are raising the kill reward.



Wards
   Cooldown (          ): 1 → 10


We found that being able to ward every round allowed champions to essentially sweep
bushes very quickly which is not intended. By increasing the ward cooldown placement
has to have a little more thought behind it.



Altar Lock HP
    Health (    ): 3 → 5


Many champions have a form of attack reset which lets them clear altar locks incredibly
quickly. We hope to remedy this by increasing altar lock hp slightly.




Breaking SDK details
With the new balance patch, the SDK has changed adding new Enum values for both the
new camps and champions. Adjustments to the dash ability data structure has also been
made adding ability_dash / AbilityDash .


Old submissions that relied on these patterns will now fail to compile. The teams with
these submissions will be removed from matchmaking until they upload a new working
submission.
