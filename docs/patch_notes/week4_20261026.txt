New emblem families:
  Tribute     tribute time reduced by 33% (rounded up, minimum one turn)
  Capture     capture time 5 -> 3 turns (when at least one allied capturer owns it as the capture begins)
  Prospector  +25% gold and gems from Gold / Gem Cluster camps only; each capturer owning it gets 25% of their own share extra, rounded down separately
  Hunter      +50 gold per champion kill (to the killer, before gold-income bonuses)
  Skirmish    +5% damage to champions
  Greed       no passive; combines at 25 shards (750 gold) to give 35 shards worth of stat points
  Collectors  +1% damage per unique completed emblem family owned (counts itself; duplicates / stat variants count once; shards do not)
System: draft first-turn fuel limit x10.
