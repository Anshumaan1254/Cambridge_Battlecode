# Patch 3.0.4 (Oct 21): what the balance change does to GOD, and the new GOD for it

Request (the user, Oct 21): "make the new bot according to these changes and also compare them on these changes only", with the
updated `GameRules.md` / `BotAPICpp.md` (now `docs/sprint/v3_0_4/`). Every number below is from engine 3.0.4 (ledger
`experiments/results/ledger_v3_0_4/`), both sides of every map; v8 = `_v8k1` (GOD v8 as shipped, rebuilt on the 3.0.4 SDK).

## 1. The patch, as the engine reports it

`tools/probe/champion_stats_main.cpp` prints `champion_designs[i].base_stats()` and every ability payload; run on the 3.0.2 and the
3.0.4 engines (`experiments/results/probe_v3_0_4/`), the only differences are the announced ones:

| Champion | 3.0.2 | 3.0.4 |
|---|---|---|
| Stellar | hp 750 (+75), focus 85 (+9) | hp 650 (+65), focus 70 (+7) |
| Heart of Ruin | str 40 (+4), dash landing 1.75 x focus | str 30 (+3.5), dash landing 1.5 x focus |
| Ren | S1 stun damage 1.25 x focus | 0.75 x focus |
| Vesper Vale | hp 900, str 50 | hp 1000, str 55 |
| Vendetta | hp 800 | hp 1000 |
| Tavi | hp 750, focus 100 | hp 900, focus 110 |
| Prism | 1.0 focus per carried gem | 1.5 (SDK constant `prism_focus_per_gem`) |

GOD reads its own kit and stats from the engine (`kit_of`, `base_stats()`, `bc::constants`): its fight, burst, kill-time and build
arithmetic follows the patch with no change. What does not follow is the champion knowledge typed into the code (ISSUES.md #37-40):
above all the draft's priors (`draft.cpp` `prior()`: Ren 1.0, Stellar 0.95, ... Tavi 0.33, Vendetta 0.3, Vesper 0.12), which put the
two nerfed champions at the top and the buffed ones near the bottom.

## 3. The new draft: measured values that follow the engine (`pd_stat`)

v8's draft keeps its whole structure (map type, first pick, greedy plan, map-fit and counter terms; `draft.cpp` `pd_score`); `pd_stat`
changes where the champion values come from and removes the names from the role terms:

- **Priors** `pd_pri_*` (and `pd_ci_prior`, `pd_hor_prior`): the values measured on 3.0.4 (§2), not v8's table. `pd_stat` 2 drops v8's
  pair (+0.3) and ranged-carry (+0.2) bonuses, which the measured values already contain (each screen kept them in place).
- **Engine anchoring**: each prior + `pd_stat_k` x ln(relative stat power now / relative stat power on 3.0.4). The stat power is computed
  at draft time from `champion_designs` (`body_power`: effective health = base health + one shield, times the geometric mean of the damage
  per round against 1 and 3 targets: basic attacks with the reset, spells modifier x focus / 8, area spells and a damaging dash x targets,
  Varg's splash, Ren's retaliation, Prism's carried-gem focus, Heart of Ruin's passive; Cassi & Issac add both bodies), relative to the
  mean of the released picks. The 3.0.4 values are the anchors (`PD_STAT_REF`), so on 3.0.4 the shift is 0 and the measured priors play
  as measured; the next balance patch moves each prior by how much it moved that champion's power against the field. On 3.0.2 the same
  formula gives (power ratio 3.0.4 / 3.0.2): Stellar 0.774, Heart of Ruin 0.804, Ren 0.929, Vesper 1.225, Tavi 1.233, Vendetta 1.253,
  everyone else 1.002: the patch's direction, read from the engine.
- **Roles from the kit, not names**: the first pick is the frontliner with the best prior (v8: Ren by name); picks are emitted in prior
  order (body indices follow it); the ranged-carry bonus (`pd_stat` 1 only) goes to whichever caster comes first.

The map rules (objective ring, open small-team maps, pack / elk shares, team size 3 Heart of Ruin) and the counters to enemy picks are
v8's: the screens kept them in place, so the values are measured with them.

## 4. The dynamic draft (`pd_stat` 3): no per-champion value anywhere

The user (Oct 21): "ensure nothing is hardcoded and everything is decided dynamically according to the maps and the enemies moves and
strategies". The measured priors of §3 are per-champion numbers; `pd_stat` 3 replaces them with a model whose inputs are read at draft
time and whose weights are fitted once on games:

- **Pick features** (`draft.cpp` `body_feats` / `pick_feats`, from `champion_designs`: base stats, ability payloads, passive constants):
  effective health (base health + one shield) / 1000; basic damage a round (strength, +1/8 with an attack reset) / 50; Varg's splash
  (`varg_splash_radius`); spell damage (damage and damaging-stun modifiers x focus, + heals) / 8 / 50; area damage (area spells, a
  damaging dash landing) / 8 / 50; stuns (stunning spells, area stuns, a stunning dash, an enhanced reset); a dash; ranged; Vesper's gold
  bonus (`vesper_vale_bonus_gold_percent`); Ren's retaliation (`ren_retaliation_focus_ratio` x focus); bodies (Cassi & Issac: 2).
- **Map interactions** (from `analyse()`: this map's camp groups on our half): area damage x the pack share, stuns x the elk share, the
  gold bonus x the gold share, bodies x a 3-a-side map, a dash x the mean group distance, range x monoliths (monolith range 5).
- **Weights** (`params.h` `pd_w_*`): `tools/draftmodel/fit.py`, logistic regression of 7,520 kept games on engine 3.0.4: win ~ side +
  w . (our team's features - theirs), team features = the sum over its picks; base stat weights kept >= 0 (more of a stat never lowers a
  pick). Fitted: health +0.51, basic damage +0.53, area damage +0.55, stuns +0.21, gold bonus +0.74, bodies -0.86 (a second linked body
  costs more than it gives), spell damage +0.03 and range 0 (our code gets little from single-target spells beyond the stun), area x
  packs +0.24, bodies x 3-a-side -0.23, dash x spread +0.15, range x monolith +0.11. Checks: 5-fold leave-maps-out log loss 0.647 vs
  0.691 for the side alone; it predicted the three round-3 cells it was not fitted on within 3 points (77.3 / 80.0 / 78.3 vs 76.9 /
  76.9 / 78.8).
- **Map types** stay v8's, in kit terms: objective ring maps add 0.75 x (the pick's spell damage from 4 / the best pick's), +0.4 for a
  melee pick with an area stun, -0.6 for other melee picks; open small-team maps -0.5 for retaliation, +0.45 for splash. The first pick
  on ordinary maps is the best frontliner by value (v8: Ren by name); later picks come out by value (body indices follow it).
- **Enemy picks**: `counter_model` (our range / stuns against their melee basic damage, our health against their burst, our dashes
  against their range, in the antisymmetric form of the fit). Its weights `pd_cw_*` need games against varied enemy drafts: the v8-only
  data cannot identify them (leave-maps-out loss 1.64), so a round-robin of 13 draft variants is being played (§5).
- **Fuel**: every pick's features are computed once in the first draft turn (1M fuel) and cached; later draft turns (100k) use 10-24k.
  (The first build recomputed them on every score: its later draft turns ran out of fuel and the engine drew the remaining picks at
  random; caught by a `DRAFT_FUEL` trace before any screen.)

**Result on the tuning half: `_m1` 256/312 = 82.1% [77.4, 85.9] vs v8, maps 2-0 110 / 1-1 36 / 0-2 10, deaths 3,481 vs 6,856** (size 5
83.7%, size 4 77.4%, size 3 84.0%): above every hand-set prior table of §2-3. Typical teams: Vendetta, Vesper, Heart of Ruin, Ryth,
Marcella at 5; Ryth, Vesper, Vendetta, Heart of Ruin at 4; Ryth, Vesper, Heart of Ruin or Vendetta at 3, the order and the members
moving with the map's packs, spread and size.
