# GOD v14 (Oct 24-25): stuttering, banking, monoliths and wards

The user (Oct 24, after GOD v13): "v12 was stuttering in between and not moving or moving randomly, or even after good gems moving away
from the altar and not contributing, also not competing with enemies in the right way. Fix the main stuttering issue and confusion.
The monoliths are not being killed in the maps: fix that, as the opponent kills them. Wards are thrown at random locations: throw them
at the bushes and use them effectively."

release mode, replays kept). The same counts on the 600 ladder replays of Oct 24 (top teams against each other) give the reference.

- **Stuttering is real**: our bodies walk back and forth 2-3 times as often as the ladder teams, v12 the most (its score multipliers,
  live bytes and capture rule move targets around more).
- **Wards are thrown at random tiles**: every 10 rounds every body re-throws its ward two tiles toward the enemy spawn (a ward there
  adds almost nothing to the body's own sight); 6% land in a bush, where a ward reveals whoever hides in it (a ward outside a bush
  does not: ENGINE_FACTS).
- **Banking with big bags is at the ladder teams' level overall.** The bad cases are specific: road-of-riches (the spiral; a 120-step
  bank trip for 21 gems after a capture-recall), boss-battle (the only altar is behind the monolith), and big bags in long chases.
- **Monoliths**: our brain kills them at the ladder rate overall (1.6 a game in our games, 1.4 on the ladder), but later (first kill
  r890-1250 on several maps where the ladder teams kill at r530-900) and never on split, fracture, great-wall, road-of-riches.
  Traced: every monolith group scores -1 in `group_score` ("too heavy for us and the bodies already on it", WHY 7) until our
  bodies are strong, because the solo plan cannot clear 20,000 health and no team plan covers monolith groups.

## 2. Causes, from the trace (LEGEND_TRACE, IDLE lines: a body that neither moved nor attacked nor cast with no enemy within 5)

80 traced games (v13 vs IC's draft): 7.9% of our body-turns are idle with no enemy near. By decision point:

| Idle turns | What it is |
|---|---|
| 10,089 | capturing (the 5-round capture timer): by design |
| 7,686 | waiting next to a respawning group: by design (camp parking) |
| 4,607 + 1,559 | **farming, no camp in attack range, the move failed or was never tried** |
| 3,881 + 1,438 | healing on spawn: by design |
| 952 | a bank trip whose step failed |

The farming idle turns, by champion and failed move: dash kits (Heart of Ruin, Vendetta, Vesper Vale) 3,300 of them; melee bodies
(Ryth, Marcella, Heart of Ruin, Vendetta) 1,550 with no move tried at all. Three mechanisms:

1. **A live camp ringed by dead tiles of its own group** (gem-fields' tTt rings, stampede): the reach goal excluded every group
   tile, dead ones included, so a melee body two tiles from the live camp had nowhere to go and stood still.
2. **A ready dash that cannot land** at a hole crossing: the wait rule waited for the dash, the dash was already up, so it waited for
   ever (the other branch walked around only when the cooldown was long).
3. **Heart of Ruin's strike dash** is held for fights near enemies; at a hole crossing with no walking route it stood still.

Back-and-forth walks: target switches 262 a game, 23% of them back to the target left within 20 rounds (A-B-A), mostly on score
differences ("better"), then waits that ran out, threats near the target; same-speed chases (eight: Ryth at 35% health with 37 gems
chased a Vesper Vale walking away for 13 rounds, then walked back).

## 3. The fixes (`bots/god14` = v13 + switches in `params.h`, section "GOD v14"; every switch 0 = v13)

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| `fz_dead` 1 | the reach goal may use dead tiles of the group (standable) when the off-group tiles are none or cannot be stepped to |
| `fz_dash` 1 / 2 | 1: a ready dash that found no landing walks around; 2: Heart of Ruin's held dash crosses when no walk leads on and nobody is within 5 |
| `fz_chase` N | a fight whose target's distance has not shrunk for N rounds out of reach ends; the target is left alone 12 rounds unless it comes into reach |
| `fz_osc` N | back-and-forth for N rounds: the target is blacklisted 20 rounds (or the bank trip dropped) and the body re-plans |
| `ward_v14` 1 / 2 | wards go into the best bush tile in reach (scored by our target group, the altar we bank at, where the enemy was seen, their side); a ward already in a bush near us stays; 1: with no bush in reach the ward stays while within 10 of us (a scouting ward only when it is far); 2: v13's forward ward then |
| `mono_v14` 1 (with `mono_team` 1) | monolith groups with harmless partners (monolith + gem clusters) are team targets, judged with their own limits (70-round clear, 1.5 health pools of hits: bodies take turns in front) |
