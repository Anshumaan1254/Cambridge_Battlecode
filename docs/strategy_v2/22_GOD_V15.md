The user (Oct 25, with four match groups of v14 against top ladder teams): "we are losing badly to the top ladder teams, mostly by gem
margin; analyse the reasons; change the strategy and the drafting; ward outputs should reach everyone through shared memory; see if

use the new strategies from their matches."

- **The gap is the middle band** (560 of the 772-gem capture gap) and it is lost by contact: with an enemy in sight our bodies stop
  farming and walk (chase, flee, rally). Without an enemy within 8 we stutter less than they do (27 back-and-forth windows vs 50).
- **Fuel is not a cause** (170 of 96,184 game turns over 100k; none on most maps).
- **The draft is not the cause**: the four teams draft the same core (Vesper Vale, Vendetta, Heart of Ruin) plus Cassi & Issac,
  Marcella or Tavi; ours is Heart of Ruin, Vesper Vale, Vendetta, Marcella, Ryth. On the 8,455 current-patch ladder games the picks add
  nothing to the rating difference (`experiments/results/mapintel/draft_model/report.md`), and in our brain our draft beats IC's draft.
- **Gold**: we earn 39% of their gold and spend it at once; the item gap is income, not the shop order.
- **Worst games**: swirl (spawns 15 tiles apart: 35 and 16 deaths, 37 and 0 gems), stampede (the only gem group is the 160-gem elk
  objective; Claudus Maximus held it, we took 69 gold-cluster captures and 0 gems), boss-battle (one central altar: 889 gems captured,
  0 banked, 584 lost at death next to it).

**Our local opponents do not play like the ladder teams.** `tools/analysis/v15_kpi.py` on 4 local games on corners (v14 and a v15
variant against `_px_ic`, IC's draft on GOD v10's brain): the proxy farms 1.1% of its rounds with an enemy within 8 and walks 79% of them;
the ladder teams farm 16.0% and walk 50%. Every local screen therefore measures us against an opponent with our own contact reflex.

**Where the walking comes from** (`tools/analysis/v15_neartask.mjs`, LEGEND_TRACE build of v14 vs `_px_ic` on corners, 2 games): with an
enemy within 8 our bodies moved 1,377 body-rounds with the task FIGHT (603 with a hit), 850 FLEE, 174 FARM. 671 of the 1,522 fight-task
walks kept the same distance to the nearest enemy with no hit in the next 3 rounds: same-speed chases that `fz_chase` (keyed to one target
handle) misses when the target changes.

## 2. The changes (`bots/god15` = v14 + switches in `params.h`, section "GOD v15"; every switch 0 = v14)

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| `g15_calm` bits | a threat = an enemy that can hit us next turn, closed in on us since our last turn, or appeared within 6. 1: pre-emptive flee exits need a threat; 2: safety()'s burst retreat counts threats only; 4: a fight we must walk to needs a threat, a kill, a channel or a 40+ bag; 8: rallies are not answered while working a group |
| `g15_fwalk` N | fight-walk budget: N turns in a row walking to a fight or a rally without getting into reach turn proactive fights and rallies off for `g15_fwalk_off` (12) rounds |
| `g15_lock` bits | 1: every body carries an altar lock from its first shop visit; 2: an enemy tribute of 20+ gems is posted at its altar with the rounds it has left (bit 7 of bytes 2-5, units of 4); 4: lock run: a lock carrier that reaches placement range in time walks there and places the lock (it cancels the channel); 8: place it on a posted altar without seeing the tributer |
| `g15_sight` 1 (with `om_sight` 1) | shared sightings from every body and its ward anywhere on the map (each body sees only itself and its own ward) |
| `g15_stance` | fixed team stance (1 = grouped) instead of the bandit |

Shared memory with `g15_lock` 2 (8 bytes): bytes 0-5 claims (bits 0-5) + team bag (bit 6) + stance (bit 7 of bytes 0-1); **bit 7 of bytes
2-5 = tribute rounds left / 4**; bytes 6-7 the team alert (tile + kind). The alert slot carries RALLY / OPPORTUNITY / DANGER (sightings);
a posted tribute stays up until it lands (sightings and rallies do not overwrite it).
