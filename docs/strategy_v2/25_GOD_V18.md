## 2. The ladder now (`experiments/ladder_oct26_v17/`, analysis scripts and tables in `analysis/`)

16 of v17's latest ranked losses and 10 recent ranked wins each of IC and Mite were downloaded and decoded (`report_oct26.txt`).

- **Mirror drafts.** The teams that beat us draft what we draft: 4 of the 16 games were exact mirrors, 7 more shared 4 of 5
  champions and 3 more 3 of 4 (their picks: Heart of Ruin 15 of 16, Vesper 13, Ryth 13, Marcella 12, Vendetta 11, Willow 3, Varg 3,
  C&I 2, Ren 1). The game is decided by play, not the draft. IC plays Vesper, Vendetta, Heart of Ruin, Cassi & Issac, Marcella; Mite
  Vesper, Vendetta, Heart of Ruin, Tavi, Marcella (neither picks Ryth).
- **The gap is camp hits, not damage per hit.** Per game: camp damage 104k vs 143k, camp hits 922 vs 1,348, damage per hit 113 vs
  106. Our Ryth, Marcella and Heart of Ruin hit camps 108-131 rounds a game against 155-196 for the same champions on the other side.
- **Where the rounds go** (body-rounds a game, us / them): walking with an enemy within 8: 1,039 / 772; camp hits 633 / 943; standing
  (captures, waits) 604 / 887; dead 667 / 638. Walking without an enemy near is equal (2,059 / 1,999).
- **Contact while farming** (a body that hit a camp last round and now has an enemy within 6): they keep farming 49% of the time
  (IC 46%, Mite 52%), we 21%; we go to fight 49% (they 33%, IC 30%, Mite 27%) and walk off 22% (they 7%). Farming through contact
  is not deadlier (death within 12 rounds 5-11% farming, 9-14% fighting). They meet us 13 times a game while farming, we meet them 5.
- **Chasing when ahead.** With our local health 1.2x theirs, 42-44% of our near-enemy turns step toward the nearest enemy (they
  26-27%, IC 27%, Mite 22%); they hit camps 33-35% of those turns, we 23%. Our chase episodes take 430 body-rounds a game to their 330
  for the same kills.
- **The middle.** Captures in the band where the two spawns are within 4 tiles of equal distance: IC 15.7 and Mite 18.1 a game (their
  opponents 10.8); in our losses we take 3.0 there and they 6.9, and they take 4.5 groups a game on our half (we 0.6 on theirs).
- **Builds.** The strong teams buy Treasure first (IC 12, Mite 17 shards by r200, even after the nerf), then Jungle and Bruiser
  stacks (Bruiser = 7 shards of stats for 5); we lead with Monster (right after the buff). Their shard totals are higher because
  they farm more.
- **Not strategies.** The large `Set priority` values (805306376, 134217736, ...) are bit-packed messages to their own champions
  (priority is readable for visible champions); priority only orders a team's own turns. Our opening loses rounds 1-2 to the init
  fuel (up to 7 on islands), but acting 2 rounds later changes nothing locally (`_sr5` 71/140, +17).

Contact discipline in local games (`contact.py` on 10 games vs v17): a body that meets an enemy while farming keeps farming 23% of the
time (v17 12%; the ladder's strong teams 46-52%) and goes to fight 45% (v17 53%). It moves us toward the strong teams' reflex without the
deaths of the never-flee proxy.

**Opening fuel** (`g18_fast_init`): `World::init_static` spent up to ~255k fuel on islands in one altar-approach lookup (every approach
tile against every camp of every group) and ~180k on the per-hole dash scan; it now looks up camp tiles only and builds the hole crossings
from shifted bitboards (checked equal to the scan on islands, ambush, lanes, starfish, geodes). First moves: islands round 4 (v17 7-9),
lanes 4 (9), ambush 3 (3-6), starfish and dunes 2 (3-5).

## 4. The draft and the patch's fuel

- The draft reads every champion's numbers from the engine (`champion_designs`, `kit_of`): the buffs and nerfs reach the pick model
  and every fight estimate without code changes. What was learned before the patch is the strong teams' pick prior (`mi_pick`): off in v18.
- The first draft turn now has 10M fuel (was 1M; our turn 1 used up to 989k): every guarded block now runs on turn 1. The DL pick model
  (T2 champion values) never beat constant values held out (log loss 0.668 vs 0.622), so fuel was not what kept it out. The one deep model
  that beat its bar is the graph-attention next-target model (top-1 0.381 vs the forest's 0.360), too heavy for a game turn; with 10M it
  could run once in the draft as a table, but its weights were never exported and it learned from the old patch: it needs the user's new
  DL run on current-patch data (doc 26) with the graph model exported.
