BUSHES AND EMBLEMS vs THE TOP-30 (stream bush_emblem, Nov 3 2026)          scripts + tables: experiments/top30_nov3/bush_emblem/
==========================================================================================================================================
Data. Bushes: the fights stream's rows (fights/out/{top,our}_{games,fights,deaths,retreats}.jsonl, same fight / win definitions:
win = more deaths, else >=1.3x damage and >=150) joined to positions + bush tiles from dec/ + mj/ (bush_extract.py -> out/*_bush.jsonl;
159 top-30 vs top-30 games, 223 of ours of which 91 v88). Emblems: economy/rows_{top30,ours}.jsonl builds (656 top-30 team-games,
305 ours v84-v88 + their opponents). Sides: TW / TL = winning / losing top-30 side; US / OPP = us / our opponent in the same games
(loss-heavy: compare US with OPP and with TL); US88 = v88 only. Map class by bush share of passable tiles: lo <3%, mid 3-6%, hi >=6%.
Tables: bush_tables.txt (A openings, B deaths, C presence, D retreats, E per champion, F stratified), retreat_strat.txt,
emblem_tables.txt, state_phase.txt, marc_fights.txt.

HEADLINE. Bushes are a small lever: at equal items and numbers, opening an exchange from a bush adds +1.4 points of win share
in the top-30 games (n 609 vs 1839; TW -2.5, TL +6.6), +3.1 pooled over all sides; we already open from bushes about as often as the
top-30 (21.9% vs TW 23.4 / TL 25.5 / OPP 21.4) and are killed from bushes less often than they are (20.1% vs 25%). The one bush rule with a
real effect is RETREATING into a bush (deaths within 8 rounds -7 to -12 points at equal hp, power ratio and map class). Emblems are
the bigger lever, and the gap is one champion: the top-30 Marcella builds FOCUS (74% of her first 20 shards), ours builds 0% focus,
and with the shard gap and body counts held, exchanges with a focus Marcella are won +9.4 points more often (top-30) / +9.1 (our
2000-rated opponents). Top-30 build choices depend on the phase, not on gem lead, enemy roster or map (all within +-10 points).

NOT GAPS (checked; do not spend time here)
- Being ambushed: our champion deaths with the killer in a bush in r-3..r-1: 20.1% (n=1963) vs top-30 25.1-25.2% (n 813 / 1919);
  hi maps 24.1% vs 33-37%. Carriers killed from a bush: ours 17.8% (n=439) vs 20-22%. Our kills from bushes (OPP deaths) 21.0%.
- Opening from bushes: US 21.9% of openings vs TW 23.4 / TL 25.5 / OPP 21.4 (hi maps 31.7 vs 30.5 / 34.2).
- Build state-dependence: within a phase the top-30's FIGHT share moves +6-10 pts when ahead in gems (p2 47% vs 37-41%) and not at all
  when behind; Durability / Jungle share 10-17% whatever the enemy roster (Marcella, 2+ ranged, other); ECON share the same on
  gem-scarce and gem-rich maps (18-26%). A gem-lead or roster rule has nothing to copy; the phase order and Marcella's stat do.
- Shop timing: 80-89% of shop trips are recalls for every side (respawn trips ~0-1%); we spend faster (unspent gold per body at
  r300 16-23 vs TW 35-42, OPP 36-46; at r600 20-25 vs 37-68): the item gap is income (plan E4), not hoarding.
- What they do with gold when behind: nothing different (unspent gold and families the same as when even).

PROPOSED BUILD RULE (for the user's "state-dependent" ask): the data supports a phase rule, not a gem-lead rule:
  r <= ~250: first emblem in an ECON family in the champion's damage stat (Marcella FOCUS, Vesper / Vendetta / Varg strength, HoR
  focus); r 250-400: Jungle / Bruiser; after ~400: Bruiser / Power / Durability. Implement as S1 (Marcella) first, S5 second, each its
  own switch; leave the gem-lead / roster hooks out (no measured effect to copy).
