DATA AND DEFINITIONS
- confusion/flips.py: 137 v88 games (ours, 132 losses), 400 top-30 vs top-30 games (top_W / top_L = 400 sides each), 70 old v84-87
  games. Output: out/fl/<key>.json; summary: summ.py -> out/summary.txt; trajectory pass traj.py -> out/traj_{v88,top}.jsonl
  (all our episodes, a 10% sample of top episodes); trq.py / bagq.py / bag2.py / exsel.py are the queries quoted below.
- WASTED LOOP: a body is back within 1 tile of where it stood k = 2..12 rounds earlier, with >= 2 moves and no productive action in
  between (attack, non-dash cast, camp hit, capture, submit / bank, shop, teleport, lock). Each move inside such a loop is a wasted
  round; contiguous wasted rounds = 1 episode. This covers A-B-A, reversals and walk-toward-X-then-back. The g17_bait recall is not
  a productive action and not a move, so it neither creates nor hides a loop. Each episode is classified by the state at its first
  round: FIGHT (champion hit given or taken within +-2: kiting; other stream), ENEMY6 (nearest enemy <= 6), ENEMY12 (7..12),
  CAMPAGGRO, RECALL (held recall), BAG (>= 30 gems), LOWHP (< 45%), SPAWN (<= 4 of own spawn), DEADGRP / LIVEGRP (within 4 of a
  dead / live group), OPEN (otherwise).
- ACTIVITY per body-round: dead > bank > recall > capture > fight > camp > spawn > walk_bag (>= 40 gems) > walk > wait.
