 number: of 118 ME88 losses, 11 reached r2000 (9.3%, against 3.6% of top-30 sides). In 1 of them banked + carried beat the winner:
   e36c7dd7 02the_pond, lost 843-846 with 244 carried (top_L: 3 of 305 such games).
 examples: e36c7dd7 HeartOfRuin b5 (we are team 1). Bag 60 at r1850, 215 at r1958. Our banked reached 831 at r1961, so 831 + 215
   >= 1000 made the bag "winning" from r1961; tribute()'s endgame flag (round + dist + ticks + 12 >= 2000) was already set by about r1950.
   Recall events every round r1940-1991, with Attack r1940-1964 and dashes r1976 / r1984; at (2,29) at r1976. Submitted 232 gems at
   the (3,7) altar at r1992; 24 ticks lands at r2016, after the end.
 cause (two candidate paths; not traced turn by turn):
   (1) tribute() (brain.cpp l.6013), v27_avoid branch: "if (P.v27_avoid > 0 && round < av_until && !recall_bank && !force) { ... if
       (hot) { if (W.dist_home(pos) > P.recall_min_dist && ... safe_to_recall()) recall_bank = true; else return false; } }". This sets
       recall_bank even when endgame or winning is set (v27_avoid = 12). go_home() then recalls, and camp hits or the next move cancel
       the recall.
   (2) The Attack events show that combat or farm ran before tribute() in r1940-1964, so the bag was never put first.
   Nothing falls back to walking after repeated failed recalls.
 fix: g24_bank_fallback (default 0), in tribute(). The v27_avoid hot-altar branch does not apply when endgame or winning is set. If a
   recall_bank recall has been cancelled >= 3 rounds in a row, drop recall_bank and walk to the nearest altar (go_altar). In turn(), a
   winning or endgame bag calls tribute(true) before farm and camp combat (enemy combat keeps priority).
 test: on local games, a mechanism log of "recall cancelled >= 3 times while winning" should drop to 0.
