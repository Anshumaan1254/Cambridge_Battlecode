WHAT THE SIX LEADS TURNED OUT TO BE
  L1 equal-R exchanges: an item gap, not a fight rule. Opener at R 0.67-1.5: win US 51% vs OPP 61% (n 418 / 369), but the mean shard
     gap per participant is -6.0 (US) vs +5.6 (OPP). With the gap held within +-3 shards: US 58% (146), OPP 57% (118), TOP 65% (130).
     Receiver at R 0.67-1.5, gap within +-3: US 35% (122), OPP 41% (160), TOP 36% (151). In the same band our hits deal 83 damage
     each vs 105 for the hits we take (TOP 118/119). We land more hits (15.1 vs 12.2 a side) for the same total damage. Our side
     lands 0.57 damage-spell hits an exchange vs OPP 1.31 / TOP 1.21. Marcella is our only damage-spell body, and she casts as often as
     the top-30 Marcellas (14.6 vs 14.4 spell hits per 1000 rounds alive), so that is the draft, not combat(). Target choice is the same:
     share of hits on the most-hit target 0.85 (US) vs 0.80 (OPP) vs 0.83 (TOP); distinct targets 1.64 / 1.91 / 1.78. combat() already
     reads item-adjusted strength and health (world.cpp:539 e.str = info.strength). The economy stream owns the gap (counterfactual,
     tables Q6: gap to 0 = +4.0 pts exchange win share). The fight-code findings are the escape bug S1 and the entry rules S2-S4.
  L2 answer latency is real but worth little: received exchanges answered within 1 round US 48% vs OPP 59% (2117 / 2274). The gap is
     in spells cast from 4+ tiles (answered 31% vs 48%) and on our Vesper (39% vs 52%). Answering fast moves the receiver's win share by
     only +5 pts (US 37% vs 32%; OPP 45% vs 41%): about 0.06 exchanges a game. See S6.
  L3 stuns: no like-with-like gap. Stunned in the last 3 rounds before death: US 41% vs OPP 40%; US88 39% vs OPP88 35%. TOP's 28%
     reflects which champions do the killing: killer Marcella 36% (US) vs 24% (TOP), but killer HoR, Ryth and Ren stun 60-84% of their
     victims in every group, and our deaths come more from those kits. What differs is how a stunned victim got there: walked in (2+ steps in the last 8 rounds) 59% (US) vs 40% (OPP), 47% (TOP);
     chased the killer 35% vs 21%. Part of S2.
  L4 walk-ins confirmed: S2. L5 camps the enemy woke confirmed: S4. Deaths soon after respawn are worse on v88: S3.
  L6 map size: the death gap is on small and mid maps (same games): small (< 1600 passable) US 11.7 vs OPP 8.8 deaths/game, mid 9.3 vs
     7.2, big 4.0 vs 6.9 (n 87 / 85 / 33 team-games). US88 small 13.4/g. Every fix below acts where contact is frequent; test it on the
     small-map pool first.

S1 Dashers do not dash out of a fight they leave; Heart of Ruin never does [BUG] est. gain 0.45 deaths/g = ~6 gems/game
   number: retreats with our own dash ready, dash used within 0..+2 rounds: US 38% (n 2607) vs OPP 71% (2434), TOP 74% (2790), TOPl
     75%. By champion: Heart of Ruin 12% (1343) vs OPP 68% (625), TOP 70% (967); Vesper 64% vs 75/81%; Vendetta 68% vs 70/74%.
     Caught (dead within 8 rounds), same rows: ours with the dash 10%, without 25%; at hp < 50%: dash used 33% vs OPP 77%, caught
     with the dash 15% vs without 41% (OPP 16% / 27%). Caught after a retreat without the dash: about 1.9 deaths a game (HoR 1.2,
     Vesper/Vendetta 0.7; retreats within 10 rounds of each other counted once). US88 the same (37% used, HoR 12%).
   examples (v88, story.py):
     5b7f0c2e-728a-462b-bd48-786e8e3d0049 r371-377 body 1 (HoR, 230/1100 hp, 21 gems): walks one tile per round with Marcella 2 tiles
       behind it for 6 rounds, dash ready the whole time, never casts it, dies r377 to Marcella's spell.
     f39bc1ca-e3c4-4cff-bda0-314270b30060 r746 body 3 (HoR, 57% hp, retreat from Marcella, dash ready, dead within 8).
     2ef5ac90-968a-4b05-861e-97644ecf254e r848 body 2 (Vendetta, 19% hp, chaser 1 tile away, dash ready, not used, dead within 8).
   cause:
     (a) brain.cpp Brain::mob_dash() (l.2495): "if (kit.dash_mod > 0.f && !strike_dash && enemy_near_d <= P.strike_dash_hold) return
         -1;" with strike_dash_hold = 9. Every flee step goes through go_home_step() (l.2650 "Nav::step_field(pos, W.home, 0, avoid ?
         &av : nullptr, mob_dash(), can_move, W.ally_spawn)"), so a HoR fleeing within 9 tiles of an enemy can never dash.
     (b) Yet combat()'s escape test counts that dash. brain.cpp combat() l.4239: "const bool can_escape = on_spawn || !chaser ||
         (ready(kit.dash) && !chaser_dash) || ...". A HoR caught by a same-speed chaser therefore gets can_escape = true. That skips
         the cornered trade ("else if (in_reach && !can_escape && adv >= P.cornered_margin) fight = true", l.4305) and lets
         ml2_leave / low_hp drop the fight. HoR then walks home one tile per round and is hit every round: the bot's model and its
         execution disagree.
     (c) Vesper / Vendetta: nav.cpp try_dash() (l.285) refuses every landing tile in `avoid` (= danger | camp_danger | alert_zone,
         brain.cpp l.2648) and every dash that gains fewer than 2 home-field steps (l.301). In flight, every tile within 2 is usually
         still in some enemy's danger zone, so the dash is skipped and the body walks.
   fix g24_flee_dash (params.h, default 0). The hook is combat()'s two flee branches (in-reach flee before "if (toward_help() ||
     go_home_step(true))" at ~l.4764, and the outmatched flee at ~l.4815) plus g23_leave(). Rule: when the dash is ready and a chaser
     could hit us after a walk step (a melee enemy within 3, +2 if its dash is ready, or a ranged enemy within its range + 1), cast the
     dash, HoR included (ignore strike_dash_hold while task == T_FLEE). Landing tile within 2: fewest enemies whose next-turn hit
     reach (attack range + 1 for melee, + 2 with dash ready, 4 with a damage/stun spell ready) covers it, then the shortest
     W.dist_home. A tile in `danger` is allowed when it is the best one, but never a sleeping-camp tile (wakes_sleeper) or an awake
     camp's range. Bit 2: can_escape counts our dash only when g24_flee_dash is on or the kit has no strike dash (dash_mod == 0), so
     the escape model matches what the flee step can do.
     Not on the ledger: the failed "disengage" / "leave step" changed WHEN we leave. This changes HOW we leave once combat() has
     already chosen to.
   test (local games, mechanism): retreats with own dash ready that dash within 2 rounds: HoR 12% -> >= 60%, Vesper / Vendetta
     64-68% -> >= 80%. Dead within 8 rounds after a retreat started under 50% hp: 41% -> <= 30%. HoR deaths/game -0.3 (now 2.68 on
     US88). extract2.py retreats.jsonl fields dash / own_dash / died8 measure all three on local replays.

S2 Bodies walk into stronger groups: the contest rule gives a group up only at R < 0.3, and rescue counts bodies [STRATEGY]
   est. gain 0.4-0.6 deaths/g = ~6 gems/game
   number: champion deaths after 2+ steps toward the nearest enemy in the last 8 rounds: US 5.45/g (60% of champion deaths) vs OPP
     2.72/g (38%), same games; US88 5.98/g vs OPP88 1.92/g; TOPl 6.17/g (51%). Steps toward the nearest enemy within 6 at local power
     "weak" (R < 0.67): US88 52.9/g (die within 6 rounds 13%) vs OPP88 30.1/g (5%). Same, while having more bodies there
     ("weak+nmore"): US88 8.0/g (die6 17%) vs OPP88 0.8/g; with equal bodies ("weak+neq"): 24.6/g (12%) vs 12.7/g (4%). Both rose from
     v84 to v88: weak+nmore 4.1 -> 8.0/g, weak+neq 21.0 -> 24.6/g, die6 8% -> 12%. Dives (2+ steps in within 4 rounds at R < 0.67):
     US 11.6/g, deaths 1.58/g vs OPP 6.9/g, 0.53/g.
   examples:
     6fa9287c-7482-447e-813c-e8aefb376e90 r703-709 body 1 (Vesper 1000 hp, allies HoR / Ryth 1500): 5 steps toward an enemy Vendetta
       (3000 hp) and HoR (3313 hp) farming at (17,14). Local R 0.39 with 2 bodies vs 2, dead r709. This is a farm walk into their

     fd46f2d5-190f-4ca9-960f-dd49fe076a2e r1072 body 5 (HoR, full hp, allies 1.2 vs enemies 0.8 within 6, R 0.24, killed by
       Marcella).
     2bbe045f-7bc7-403d-87a3-69d06a3f92c2 r769 body 7 (Marcella, full hp, 2 vs 2, R 0.11, killed by Vesper).
   cause:
     (a) brain.cpp group_score() l.7240: "if (tadv < (P.v23_contact ? P.v23_abandon_r : P.target_threat_adv) * (big ? 0.5f : 1.f))
         return -1;" with v23_contact 1 and v23_abandon_r 0.3: a group with stronger enemies near it is dropped only at odds below

     (b) g23.cpp g23_veto() rescue (bit 64, live): "if (n_en > n_us) continue;". The rescue fights whenever our bodies within 4 of the

   fix: two switches, default 0:
     g24_abandon_r = 0.67: group_score drops a group whose tadv < g24_abandon_r (x0.5 for big groups, unchanged), unless the group is
       our task group and nobody can hit us next turn (g23_keep_farm).
     g24_rescue_pw = 1: in g23_veto's rescue, also require the power ratio at the ally, g23_ratio(a.pos, E, ...) >= g23_r_join (0.85),
       besides n_en <= n_us.
     Not the failed dive filter: that one blocked every step closer at R < 0.67, fights and retreat corridors included (-22). This
     only removes (a) a farm target and (b) a rescue that loses on power. combat()'s own engagement bars are untouched.
   test: local small-map pool: steps toward at R < 0.67 with >= equal bodies (extract2 step_stats weak+neq + weak+nmore) 32.6/g ->
     <= 20/g; walk-in champion deaths -0.5/g. Rescue fights started (G23_TRACE) keep >= 70% of today's count (rescue was +31 held-out:
