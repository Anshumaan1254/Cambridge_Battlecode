   examples: 603f9ead-f23f-4263-a4de-d722dbd0a774 (dromedaries, 00oasis, 162-1012): their Marcella = body 7 captures r24 (22 gems,
     90 gold) and banks r33, r56 -> r57, r85 -> r87, r111 -> r121, r312 -> r318; 17 shards at r300, 87 at r600 while our best body has 19;
     she kills 22 of ours (r262 b2 HoR, r346 b0 Vendetta, r368 b3 Ryth). 4032595e-7f6e-4245-af47-1e77e1196f23 (KKB, 00gem_fields, 566-1007):
